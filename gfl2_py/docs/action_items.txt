21. stat_ocr_fft: '2's bottom-band stroke-thickness measurement likely
    conflates curve extent with bar thickness, not yet investigated

   STATUS: OPEN

   CONTEXT: 2026-07-10, debugs/debug_line_stroke_thickness_sweep.py's
   corpus sweep (built to derive a physically-grounded stroke-thickness
   buffer for the LINE-SPLIT SOBEL MODE's '7' isolation gate, and to flag
   outlier images along two variance axes -- centroid shift and stroke
   thickness -- for the separately-requested top-20 meaningful-image
   curation). measure_stroke_thickness() counts consecutive rows within a
   digit-specific row band whose foreground occupancy clears BAR_INK_FRAC
   (0.5) of the glyph's width.

   ANOMALY: '7' and '5' (both straight top bars) measured a tight,
   consistent thickness across the whole 87-image corpus (3.696+/-0.46px
   and 3.678+/-0.467px respectively -- std under 0.5px either way). '2'
   (bottom band, 0.60-1.0 of height) measured 5.472+/-1.846px -- roughly
   4x the variance of the other two, despite all three using the exact
   same measurement mechanism and BAR_INK_FRAC threshold. SUSPECTED
   (NOT CONFIRMED): '2's bottom band captures its curved foot/tail, not a
   straight bar the way '7'/'5's top bands do -- a curve's extent through
   a fixed-height row band is a fundamentally different (and more
   variable) quantity than a straight bar's thickness, which would explain
   the inflated variance without it being a corpus-quality or measurement
   bug.

   NOT DONE: visually inspecting a handful of real '2' glyphs' bottom-band
   masks (same "unit debug before/alongside integration" convention this
   project uses elsewhere, e.g. debugs/debug_gabor_features.py) to confirm
   whether the row-band is genuinely catching a curved foot rather than a
   flat bar; if confirmed, '2's row band and/or the ink-fraction threshold
   likely need their own dedicated tuning rather than reusing '7'/'5's
   convention as-is. This was NOT blocking for the '7'-isolation gate work
   that motivated the sweep (which only needed '7's own measurement to be
   sound, and it independently validated cleanly -- see docs/decisions.txt
   for the full write-up) -- filed here so '2's number isn't mistaken for
   an equally-trustworthy stroke-thickness figure without this caveat.

28. gfl2/stat_ocr_dp.py's classify() tree has no confidence-based
    abstention -- it always answers, even at its narrowest real margin

   STATUS: OPEN -- flagged directly as a significant design gap.

   CONTEXT: every leaf in classify() always returns a specific digit; the
   only '?' path is one defensive branch the real corpus never hits.
   Unlike gfl2/stat_ocr.py (PROJ_CORR_MIN) and gfl2/stat_ocr_fft.py (two
   independently-gated agents), this engine has no notion of "the margin
   here is thin, don't guess."  Concretely: the '2'/'5' leaf's real corpus
   margin is exactly 1 unit, and synthetic perturbation testing found
   '2'/'5' failures dominate the engine's remaining fragility under
   stress, every one a CONFIDENT wrong answer (docs/takeaways.txt #69).

   ACTION: add a real margin/confidence check per leaf and return '?' when
   a glyph falls inside that margin, mirroring gfl2/stat_ocr.py and
   gfl2/stat_ocr_fft.py's existing discipline.  Likely more durable than
   further per-leaf recalibration.

   PARTIAL PROGRESS: one leaf (the {2,3,5} split) gained a reachable
   abstention path -- see docs/decisions.txt #81.  No other leaf has any
   confidence gate; the general ACTION above remains open.
